Our Story

A Chef. A View. A Philosophy.

Chef Matteo Russo grew up along the Ligurian coast, where meals were slow, ingredients were honest, and the sea was always present. After years in Michelin-starred kitchens across Europe, he returned to the water to open Mare — a place where simplicity is the highest form of craft.

Every plate begins with what arrived that morning: fish pulled from local boats, vegetables from the hillside farms above the bay, herbs grown on our terrace. Nothing more, nothing less.
